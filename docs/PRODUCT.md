# Product: TALKBACK

## Who and why
- One user, an Israeli woman with basic English who freezes when she has to speak.
- Goals: fluent spoken American English for travel, and for work as a designer (meetings, presenting her work).
- Hebrew UI, addressed in the feminine. Microcopy: friendly, funny, a little teasing, and always with the next step.

## The method
1. **Recasts:** correct during the conversation only errors that hurt meaning, and tense errors. Do it by repeating the sentence correctly, without stopping the flow. All other errors go to the summary, behind "all errors".
2. **Comprehensible input:** speak at her level, plus a few new words and phrases.
3. **Spaced repetition:** words from her own conversations come back right before she forgets them.
4. **Shadowing:** listen and repeat for pronunciation, as a separate practice.
5. **American slang:** woven into the conversations, plus a dedicated practice.

## Personality from onboarding
8 questions, scored on: cynicism, patience, perfectionism, confidence, humor. The profile drives:
- Tone and how much the characters tease (gentle, friendly, no mercy).
- Explanation length.
- Pace.
- The recommended character.

## Daily practices (the daily path, about 20 minutes, 80 XP)
| Practice | Goal | XP |
|---|---|---|
| The character calls you | spontaneous response, 3 minutes | 20 |
| Follow-up conversation | the character remembers yesterday and asks about it. Shows up automatically when there's something to follow up | 20 |
| 60 seconds on... | fluency: words per minute, pauses, "umm" count | 10 |
| Voice diary of yesterday | past tense | 15 |
| Shadowing | pronunciation and accent | 15 |
| Describe your design | English for work. Weekly bonus | 20 |
| Slang of the day | 3 phrases, then use one in a conversation | 10 |

## Coming back every day
- **Streak:** a day counts once the daily goal is met.
- **Reminder:** from a character, in her personality, at a time she picks.
- **Memory:** characters remember what she said and ask about it.
- **Day complete:** a celebration screen, an unlocked item (wardrobe), and tomorrow's reminder time.
- **Weekly:** "what you rocked this week", and what to work on.
